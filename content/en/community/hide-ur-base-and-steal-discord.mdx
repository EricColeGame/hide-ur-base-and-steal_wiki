### 1. Strategic Base Hiding
Your portal marker is your weakest point. If an enemy steps onto it, they gain full access to your underground base. 

* **Avoid Spawns:** Never drop your portal near the default player spawn zone.
* **Use Terrain Foliage:** Place markers beneath bushes, behind rocks, or along obscure map borders.
* **Match Textures:** Community reports suggest dropping markers on uneven terrain reduces visual silhouettes.
