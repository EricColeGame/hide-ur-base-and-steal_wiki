## Masterclass: Where and How to Conceal Your Base

Your secret marker is the single point of failure for your entire empire. Placing it in plain sight on common walking paths is an open invitation for thieves. Community reports emphasize thinking three-dimensionally when dropping your entrance.

```
Good Spot:   [ Foliage / Deep Shadow ] ──> [ Base Marker ] <── Hidden behind terrain
Bad Spot:    [ Open Pathway ] ───────────> [ Base Marker ] <── Visible to all runners
```

| Location Type | Stealth Rating | Traffic Level | Pro Tip |

### Stealth Placement Rules

- **Break the Silhouette:** Never place your marker in flat, open fields. Use natural terrain contours, rocks, and environmental decorations to blend the entrance marker into the background.
- **Monitor the Spawn:** When dropping your base, make sure no other player is actively following or observing you. If someone trails you, rotate to a dummy location before committing your actual portal.
- **Relocate When Compromised:** If an intruder enters your hideout, treat that location as burned. Pick up and reposition your marker across the opposite side of the map as soon as you clear the threat.
