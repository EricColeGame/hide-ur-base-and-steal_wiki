### Designing the Switchback Maze

The Switchback Maze utilizes narrow corridors connected by alternating right and left turns. Because Roblox third-person cameras struggle around tight interior walls, intruders have limited forward visibility. If you place internal props or walls in a serpentine pattern, invaders cannot simply scan the room from the entrance marker; they are forced to explore every corner manually, dramatically increasing their risk of getting caught.

### Implementing Decoy Chambers

Do not place your highest-value pets directly at the end of the primary corridor. Instead, build a fork early in your subterranean blueprint. Place low-tier, inexpensive pets in a clearly visible front room while keeping your true revenue engines tucked behind a hidden side turn. Player experience reveals that most raiders will grab the first item they see and run, sparing your highest-yielding companions from theft.
