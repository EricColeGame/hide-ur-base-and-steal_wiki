## Stealth Concealment: How to Completely Hide Your Base

## Advanced Strategies: Executing the Hit and Fade

Stealing doors while keeping your home position secret requires careful execution. If a victim follows your trail of breadcrumbs, breadcrumb coordinates, or death logs, your hidden installation will be compromised within hours.

### Step-by-Step Tactical Raid Procedure

Follow this operational flow to secure entrance assets without burning down your stealth rating:

1. **Reconnaissance:** Scout the perimeter during off-peak server hours. Identify blind spots in turret tracking, line-of-sight gaps, and unmonitored entry ways.
2. **Entry Point Selection:** Never breach the main gate directly if secondary patio doors or roof hatches are exposed. 
3. **Silent Dismantling:** Break the blocks supporting the door frame. Often, destroying the anchor block pops the door into an inventory item instantly, avoiding loud hacking animations.
4. **Counter-Sealing:** Place a temporary, worthless block (like gravel or cobblestone) in the doorframe to confuse defenders during their initial inspection.
5. **Egress Via Dead Drops:** Do not run directly back to your covert shelter. Stash stolen doors and loot inside a remote decoy chest, kill your character or take a convoluted path, and retrieve the items later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does "hide ur base and steal doors" mean in multiplayer survival?
The phrase refers to a classic guerrilla gameplay loop where players build stealthy, undetectable bases to protect their resources while raiding enemy settlements to strip high-value security items—specifically doors and hatches—disrupting the victim's base functionality.

### Why do raiders steal doors instead of just blowing up chests?
Doors are critical infrastructure items that require specific crafting stages to produce. Stealing them strips security airlocks instantly, leaves the base open to wandering monsters or rival scavengers, and yields valuable raw materials without using expensive explosives.

### What is the best biome to hide a base in?
Deep oceans, dense jungle canopies, snowy mountain peaks, and deep subterranean cave systems offer the highest natural concealment. Ocean floors in particular absorb sound and conceal lighting, making them optimal for subterranean construction.

### Can I protect my base if I don't use conventional doors?
Yes. Many advanced builders replace traditional doors with hidden piston walls, water elevators, gravity-block drop chutes, or teleporter portals. By eliminating visible door blocks, you remove the physical weak point altogether, giving door thieves zero targets to exploit.
