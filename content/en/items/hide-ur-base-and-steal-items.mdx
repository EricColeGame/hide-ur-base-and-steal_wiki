Any unbanked currency or pets in your temporary loot sack will drop on the floor, and you will respawn at the main overworld spawn hub upon reconnecting.

### How often should I move my base entrance?
In active lobbies with high player turnover, reposition your portal marker every 15 to 20 minutes. Moving your marker resets its visual state and forces rivals who may have marked your location to spend valuable time scouting the terrain all over again.
