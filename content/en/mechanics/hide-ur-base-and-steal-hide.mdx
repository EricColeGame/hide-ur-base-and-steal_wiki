## Core Mechanics: How Bases and Markers Function

The fundamental loop of this title revolves around establishing a secure subterranean safehouse. Instead of claiming a massive, neon-lit building plot on the main surface, you carry your hideout with you. Dropping your marker creates a discreet portal point on the ground. Stepping onto this marker immediately transports your avatar into your personal underground tycoon. 

Inside this private sanctuary, you purchase income-generating pets, upgrade cash multipliers, and store your collected wealth. However, the surface marker remains physically present in the shared overworld. If another player walks over your marker, they can enter your realm and plunder your collection.

| Game Action | Mechanical Function | Player Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Drop Marker** | Deploys entrance hitbox on surface terrain | Sets the vulnerable link between map and base |

| **Base Teleport** | Steps onto your active surface marker | Takes you underground to manage pets and cash |
| **Heist Infiltration** | Walking through an enemy's hidden marker | Grants access to raid and seize rival pets |

The dual challenge lies in balancing exploration with defense. Every second spent hunting down enemy entrances leaves your own spot vulnerable unless you position your entrance with extreme care.
