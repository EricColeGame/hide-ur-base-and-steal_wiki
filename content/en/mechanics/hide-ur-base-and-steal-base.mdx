## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens when someone finds my entrance marker?
If another player finds your entrance marker, they can step directly onto it to access your private vault. Once inside, they have the ability to grab your stored pets. You can counter this by relocating your marker to another zone on the map immediately.

### Can other players steal all my pets at once?
Generally, game mechanics limit how much loot a raider can carry in a single trip. However, if a player discovers your portal and you remain away from your base for an extended period, they can make multiple trips until your hideout is cleared. Always monitor your active assets.

### What is the most effective spot to hide ur base and steal base markers?
The most reliable spots are high-elevation cliff ledges, tucked inside dense environmental geometry like multi-layered bushes, or positioned directly behind structures near the border boundaries of the map. Avoid central clearings and high-traffic routes near spawn points.
