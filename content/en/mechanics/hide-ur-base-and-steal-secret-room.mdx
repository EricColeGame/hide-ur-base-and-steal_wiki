## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What happens if someone finds my hide ur base and steal secret room?
When a player discovers your entrance marker, they can enter your vault immediately. Once inside, they have full access to pick up and steal any unguarded pets displayed on your stands. To minimize losses, always store your most valuable pets in the back of your vault behind decoy displays.

### Can I move my secret room entrance marker once it is placed?
Yes, depending on current game mechanics and tool cooldowns, you can pick up or relocate your base marker. If you suspect an enemy player followed you or knows your portal coordinate, it is best practice to relocate your entrance to an entirely new map sector.

### What is the most effective way to disguise a secret room portal?
The best disguise method is placing your marker inside dense environmental foliage, behind jagged rock formations, or beneath shadow-heavy overhangs. Avoid open, high-traffic corridors like spawn squares and central bridges.

### Do pets keep earning money while I am raiding other bases?
Yes, your pets continue to generate passive income while you roam the map scouting and raiding other players. However, leaving your vault unattended increases the risk of another player raiding your base while you are away, making solid defensive layouts essential.
