## Best Prop Archetypes for Base Placement

Not all environmental meshes provide the same degree of safety. The game map contains various categories of props ranging from outdoor park fixtures to dense indoor classroom furniture. Choosing the right archetype drastically reduces how often your base gets raided.

### 1. Benches and Seating

Benches represent the classic beginner trap. Because tutorial clips and early videos showcase players hiding under park seats, almost every beginner runs around clicking park benches first. If you decide to place your door on a bench, avoid open courtyards. Opt for rows where multiple identical benches sit close together, or find seats tucked against back walls where the interaction outline blends with level geometry.

### 2. Natural Clutter and Boulders

Natural objects like rocks, bushes, and tree trunks provide organic camouflage. Avoid solitary boulders that stand out in flat fields, as players instinctively check distinct landmarks. Instead, aim for overlapping rock piles or foliage near steep terrain where awkward camera angles make spotting the prompt difficult.

### 3. School and Institutional Interiors

Interior locations add visual clutter that works heavily in your favor. Classrooms, hallways, and administrative desks have dense arrangements of chairs, tables, and cabinets. The main hazard here is window sightlines: transparent glass reveals your avatar’s silhouette to anyone running along outside corridors.

### 4. Residential and Household Fixtures

Kitchens and bedrooms inside residential structures, like the Red House, feature distinct props including refrigerators, lamps, rugs, and wall paintings. Floor-level items like rugs naturally counteract opponents who scan the map at standard eye level.

You should move your entrance immediately whenever you notice suspicious player lingering, or right after you interrupt a thief inside your base. In high-pop servers, rotating your den every 20 to 30 minutes prevents scouting networks from mapping your routines.

### Can raiders find my base if I hide behind map boundaries?
Most map boundaries have strict collision barriers, and attempting to clip through them often results in camera bugs that reveal your exact nametag. Sticking to valid, cluttered world props inside standard gameplay zones is far safer than attempting out-of-bounds exploits.

### Do certain pets make hiding ur base and steal impossible hiding spots harder to protect?
