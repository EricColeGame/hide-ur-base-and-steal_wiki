Yes, multiple avatars can fit inside the living room or kitchen closets as long as every player stands completely within the glowing aura boundary. If any player clips outside the boundary frame, that specific player will take damage while those deep inside remain safe.

### Why did I take damage while standing right beside a hiding spot?
Proximity alone does not grant stealth protection. You must be fully inside the glowing aura until your screen visibly darkens and the purple loading circles appear. Lingering next to the edge leaves your hitbox vulnerable to enemy strikes.

### Is it better to hide ur base and steal hiding spots or climb to the upper floors?
It is always safer to use official ground-floor hiding spots or confirmed line-of-sight roosts like the refrigerator. Running upstairs traps you in dead-end bedrooms with limited escape avenues if roaming enemies decide to path up the central staircase.

### Does jumping ruin my hiding spot status?
In designated locations—especially behind the 3x3 drawer in the Entrance Room—jumping can break server registration of your hidden status.
