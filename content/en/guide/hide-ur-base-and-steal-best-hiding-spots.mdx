## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best props to hide ur base and steal best hiding spots?
The most reliable props are obscure interior decorations positioned away from room centerpieces, such as bedroom lamps and refrigerators. Large natural rocks located far outside suburban zones also make exceptional hiding spots, as most players instinctively check houses first.

### Can other players enter my base if they find the prop?
Yes. Any player who interacts with the specific prop hosting your base entrance will be transported inside your vault room. Once inside, they can loot any pet currently resting on your active display pads unless you stop them with weapons or defensive traps.

### Does server hopping instantly protect my base from looters?
No. The game features a mandatory 15-second vulnerability window after you leave the server. If an opponent is standing directly next to your entrance prop when you disconnect, they still have 15 seconds to enter and steal your pets before your vault despawns.

### How often should I move my base entrance?
Relocate your entrance immediately whenever an opponent follows you home, after a failed raid attempt on your room, or right after completing a rebirth. Moving your entrance costs nothing and prevents observant players from camping your location.
