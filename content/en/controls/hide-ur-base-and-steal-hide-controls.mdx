## Pro-Tier Movement and Camouflage Strategies

Community reports highlight several advanced tricks that high-level players use to make their bases virtually unfindable while maintaining rapid personal access.

### 1. The Shadow Align Method
Dynamic shadows cast by map assets (like trees and large rocks) create pitch-black patches on ground surfaces. If you line up your base marker directly inside these shadow pools, the visual border of your portal marker blends with the ambient lighting, making it almost invisible to passersby who do not look directly down.

### 2. Edge-Clipping the Marker
Certain structures have minor gaps between their visual model and their physical collision boundary. By nudging your character into these seams using gentle analog or WASD taps, you can drop your portal partially underneath an environmental object. The prompt remains accessible to you, but the visual indicator is hidden from view.

### 3. Sprint-Canceling the Exit Portal
When emerging from your base back into the overworld, players are momentarily vulnerable. By buffering the **Shift** (Sprint) key during the teleport loading transition, your character instantly bolts the frame they hit the surface, denying campers an easy read on your escape trajectory.

Yes, the marker is not completely invisible. It appears as a small, discreet floor decal or trapdoor on the terrain. The goal is to use natural obstacles, shadowed textures, and corners to hide the marker from direct lines of sight so players run past without noticing the interact prompt.

### How do I steal pets once I enter an enemy base?
Once you successfully use your **hide ur base and steal hide controls** to access another player's portal, walk up to their stored pets inside their base vault.
