## Troubleshooting Common Control Issues

Nothing is more frustrating than losing rare loot due to unresponsive inputs. Here are practical fixes for common control bugs reported by the community:

1. **Interact Prompt Not Triggering:**  
   If you stand directly on a base marker and the prompt fails to appear, your character model may be misaligned due to latency. Back up two paces, clear your camera obstruction, and approach at a walking pace.
2. **Sticky Mobile Virtual Stick:**  
   If your character continues drifting after you lift your thumb, open your Roblox app settings and switch the Movement Mode from *Dynamic Thumbstick* to *Classic Thumbstick*.
3. **Shift-Lock Key Conflicts:**  
   Ensure that the "Shift Lock Switch" option is enabled in the in-game settings menu; otherwise, pressing Left Shift will not lock your camera.
4. **Interact Key Lag on Controllers:**  
   Check for Bluetooth interference if you are using wireless gamepads on PC or mobile, as even a 50ms delay can ruin a theft window.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the default hide ur base and steal steal controls on PC?
The default PC setup uses W, A, S, and D for standard character movement, Spacebar to jump, Left Shift for Shift-Lock camera alignment, and the E key (or left-click) to trigger the steal and portal interaction mechanics.

### How do I steal a pet if another player is guarding it?
Wait until the defender looks away, sprint in, and hold your interact input. If they close in, cancel the theft immediately by jumping away, repositioning your camera, and waiting for another opening.

### Can I customize my control bindings inside the game?
While standard keybinds like movement and jump inherit your general Roblox client configurations, in-game user interface elements (such as shop menus and quick-inventory toggles) follow fixed layouts depending on your platform.

### Why does my base marker fail to drop when I press the action key?
Your base portal can only be dropped on valid, level ground outside of designated safe zones or other players' existing claims. If your input does nothing, move to an unobstructed surface and try again.
